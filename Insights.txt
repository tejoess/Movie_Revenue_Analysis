Movie Data Analysis - Key Insights 📊🎬
🔸 High budget doesn’t always mean high revenue.
🔸 Sci-Fi, Marvel, and Animated movies tend to perform the best.
🔸 Budget and revenue have a weak correlation.
🔸 Some low and mid-rated movies earned surprisingly well.
🔸 High-budget films don't always get higher ratings.
🔸 Movies with a duration of 100-200 minutes perform better on average.
🔸 English-language movies dominate the global box office.
🔸 Big-budget movies are rare but significantly impact total industry revenue.
